A LITTLE COMPANION FOR FARCASTER

A little help for your Farcaster world.

Fiddy helps you read, post, and open mini apps with an agent that asks before it acts.

Look around first Open fiddy

Start with a look around

Public browsing needs no sign-in. You can read the public feed and explore without an account. When you want to join in, fiddy shows you the next step.

You stay in control

Your agent asks before it acts. It explains what it wants to do, then waits for your yes. You can browse without pairing an agent, connecting a wallet, or signing in.

Straight answers

Fiddy reads through the service you choose in Settings. That can be your own setup or a hosted service you configured. If that service cannot answer something, fiddy says so in place instead of inventing a result.

This About page is just a page. It does not contact a read service, pair an agent, ask for a wallet, or require an account.
